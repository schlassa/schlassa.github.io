I'm a PhD candidate in the NYU Department of Philosophy. I teach philosophy of AI at Barnard College, and I did my undergraduate studies at Carleton College.

I work on theoretical questions about computation and representation, both biological and artificial. Mostly, I study what gives representations their structure, and what this means for their role in computation.
